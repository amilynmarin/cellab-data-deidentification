# Scientific, Privacy, and Regulatory Disclaimer

CELLab Data De-identification is research software intended to assist controlled data-release workflows. It does **not** determine or certify that a dataset is anonymous, de-identified under any particular law or regulation, safe for public release, or appropriate for a particular recipient or analytic purpose.

Use of this software does not by itself establish compliance with HIPAA, the Common Rule, GDPR, institutional policy, an IRB determination, a data-use agreement, or any other legal, regulatory, contractual, or ethical requirement. Re-identification risk depends on the source data, combinations of retained variables, external information available to recipients, the release environment, and future data sources.

Outputs therefore require appropriate human disclosure review and institutional governance. Users remain responsible for determining whether a proposed release is permitted and whether additional controls, suppression, aggregation, contractual restrictions, or security measures are required.
